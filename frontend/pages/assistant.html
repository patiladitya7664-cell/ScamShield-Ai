Immediate Safety Checklist

If you suspect that you are being targeted by a scam:

  • Do not share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, or banking credentials.
  • Do not transfer money because of threats or urgent demands.
  • Do not click suspicious links or install unknown applications.
  • Preserve relevant messages, transaction details, and other evidence.
  • Contact your bank immediately if financial information or money has been compromised.
  • Report suspected cyber financial fraud through the appropriate official channels.
