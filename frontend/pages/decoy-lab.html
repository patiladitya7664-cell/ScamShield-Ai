RESPONSIBLE AI SIMULATION

Safety Comes First

The Decoy Lab is intended for controlled cybersecurity education and fictional demonstrations.

  • Uses fictional educational scenarios.
  • Does not contact real scammers or suspicious accounts.
  • Does not perform unauthorized access or surveillance.
  • Never requests OTPs, PINs, passwords or CVVs.
  • Extracted indicators remain unverified.
  • Simulation requires explicit user consent.
  • Does not automatically start during normal fraud analysis.
