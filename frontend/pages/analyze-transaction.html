What can be detected?

Important Safety Tips

  • Never share your UPI PIN.
  • Receiving money does not require entering your UPI PIN.
  • Never share OTPs or CVV.
  • Do not install unknown screen-sharing applications.
  • Verify payment requests through official channels.
