VICTIM SAFETY

Important Safety Actions

Essential steps to take when you encounter suspicious activity or financial fraud.

Never Share OTP or PIN

Never disclose OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or banking credentials to unknown callers or messages.

Contact Your Bank

If banking credentials or payment information are compromised, contact your bank immediately.

Preserve Evidence

Preserve relevant messages, transaction records and other evidence for appropriate reporting.

INDIA CYBER FRAUD SUPPORT

Need Immediate Cybercrime Assistance?

If you have lost money due to online financial fraud, contact the Indian cybercrime helpline immediately and report the incident through the official cybercrime portal.

Call 1930 Report Cybercrime
