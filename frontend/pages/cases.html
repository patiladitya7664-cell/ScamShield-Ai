Understanding Case Status

  • Open: A case has been created and is awaiting further review.
  • Investigating: The case is being reviewed.
  • Resolved: The investigation has been marked as resolved.
  • Closed: The case has been closed.

What Can a Case Contain?

A case can organize information associated with a suspicious activity or fraud analysis.

  • Original evidence reference and evidence type.
  • Guardian detection result and risk assessment.
  • Detected fraud category and analysis summary.
  • Case priority and investigation status.
  • Relevant timestamps and follow-up information.

Case records are intended to help organize suspicious activities and investigation progress. A risk assessment is not proof of fraud. Always verify important findings through appropriate official channels.
