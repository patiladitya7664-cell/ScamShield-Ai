SAFETY TOGETHER

Family Protection

Help your family recognize suspicious requests, avoid common digital scams, and verify unexpected communications before taking action.

1. Agree on a verification step

If someone requests money urgently, contact that family member using a saved phone number or another trusted communication method.

2. Keep account codes private

Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, CVVs, or account recovery codes. Genuine support staff should never ask you to disclose these secrets.

3. Pause before making payments

Check the recipient's name, UPI ID, and amount carefully in your official banking or payment application. Never approve a collect request just to receive money.

4. Verify links independently

Open the official website or application yourself. Avoid logging in through unexpected links received through messages, emails, or social media.

5. Make it easy to ask for help

Encourage family members to discuss suspicious calls and messages with someone they trust, especially when a stranger creates fear, secrecy, or urgency.

6. Preserve useful evidence

Keep relevant screenshots, phone numbers, suspicious messages, UPI IDs, transaction references, and payment receipts. Never share passwords, OTPs, or PINs as evidence.

A SIMPLE FAMILY PLAN

Before sending money

  1. Stop and read the payment request carefully.
  2. Contact the person using a saved, trusted phone number.
  3. Independently verify the recipient and amount in your official payment application.
  4. If anything seems unusual, do not proceed until you have independently verified the request.
  5. Ask another trusted person for help if you remain unsure.

Remember: pressure, secrecy, threats, and unrealistic promises are common warning signs of scams.

IMPORTANT WARNING SIGNS

Stop and verify if someone:

  • Claims to be police, CBI, or another government authority and threatens immediate arrest.
  • Promises guaranteed investment returns or unusually high profits.
  • Requests an OTP, UPI PIN, password, or card security code.
  • Pressures you to transfer money immediately.
  • Threatens to publish private images or personal information.
  • Sends unexpected payment links or asks you to install an unknown application.

INDIA CYBER FRAUD SUPPORT

Report suspected financial cyber fraud

If you have lost money through suspected cyber financial fraud, contact the national cybercrime helpline immediately. Prompt reporting may help authorities take timely action.

About this page

Family Protection is an educational awareness resource. It does not currently provide family account linking, continuous family monitoring, automatic alerts, or real-time protection of another person's device.

The guidance provided here is for general awareness and does not replace official emergency, financial, or legal assistance.
