SAFETY TOGETHER

Family protection

Share simple safety habits with family members and agree on how to verify unusual requests before taking action.

Agree on a verification step

If someone asks for money urgently, contact that family member using a phone number you already know.

Keep account codes private

Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, CVVs, or account recovery codes, even with someone claiming to be support staff.

Pause before paying

Review the recipient and amount in the official banking app. Do not approve a payment request just to receive money.

Check links independently

Open a company’s official website or app yourself instead of using unexpected links in messages.

Make it easy to ask for help

Encourage family members to call someone they trust when a message creates fear, secrecy, or pressure to act quickly.

Save useful evidence

Keep screenshots, phone numbers, account details, and transaction references if a suspicious contact or payment occurs.

A SIMPLE FAMILY PLAN

Before sending money

  1. Pause and read the request carefully.
  2. Call the person using a saved, trusted number.
  3. Verify the recipient and amount in your official payment app.
  4. Ask another trusted person if the request still feels unusual.

This page provides general awareness guidance, not emergency or legal advice.
