How screenshot analysis works

Upload a screenshot or paste its text manually. ScamShield uses browser-based OCR to extract readable text from the image. You can review and edit the extracted text before submitting it for fraud analysis.

OCR may misread text, numbers, URLs or payment details. Always review the extracted text before analysis.

Protect your personal information

Remove OTPs, UPI PINs, ATM PINs, CVV, passwords, complete card numbers, account numbers and other sensitive financial information before submitting.
