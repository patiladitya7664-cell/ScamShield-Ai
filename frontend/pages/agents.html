MULTI-AGENT AI SYSTEM

Meet Your Four Specialized AI Agents.

Each agent has a distinct responsibility. The core analysis agents contribute to normal evidence analysis, while Decoy remains separately initiated.

AGENT 01
AUTHORITATIVE FRAUD DETECTION

Guardian Agent

Guardian is the primary fraud detection agent. It analyzes submitted evidence, identifies suspicious patterns and produces the authoritative fraud assessment.

Core Responsibilities

  • Analyze submitted digital evidence.
  • Identify suspicious patterns and signals.
  • Classify supported fraud categories.
  • Generate explainable risk assessments.
  • Preserve deterministic assessment authority.
AUTHORITATIVE OUTPUT

Fraud category, risk score, risk level, explanation and detection findings.

Explore Fraud Detection
AGENT 02
THREAT INTELLIGENCE

Intelligence Agent

Intelligence extracts and organizes useful indicators from submitted evidence to support investigation and further review.

Core Responsibilities

  • Extract phone numbers and URLs.
  • Identify UPI and email indicators.
  • Organize extracted evidence.
  • Identify possible indicator relationships.
  • Provide structured intelligence output.
PRIMARY OUTPUT

Structured indicators for investigation, review and threat intelligence.

Extracted indicators are unverified. Their presence alone does not establish that a person, phone number, email or URL is fraudulent.
Explore Intelligence
AGENT 03
VICTIM PROTECTION

Victim Safety Agent

A victim-first assistance agent that provides contextual safety recommendations for people facing suspected cyber fraud or financial scams.

Core Responsibilities

  • Provide immediate safety guidance.
  • Identify reported information exposure.
  • Recommend preserving digital evidence.
  • Guide users toward relevant bank support.
  • Explain official cybercrime reporting options.
PRIMARY OUTPUT

Contextual safety recommendations, exposure awareness and reporting guidance.

Recommendations are informational. ScamShield AI does not automatically contact banks, police or authorities.
Explore Safety Support
AGENT 04
CONTROLLED CYBERSECURITY SIMULATION

Decoy Agent

A controlled simulation agent designed for cybersecurity awareness and research through synthetic scam scenarios.

Core Responsibilities

  • Run controlled synthetic scenarios.
  • Demonstrate common scam techniques.
  • Support security awareness and research.
  • Manage isolated simulation sessions.
  • Keep simulations separate from normal analysis.
SIMULATION OUTPUT

Synthetic scenarios and controlled simulation results.

Separate Simulation Decoy is not automatically executed during normal fraud analysis. It requires an explicitly initiated simulation.
