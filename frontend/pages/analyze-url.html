Important Online Safety Tips

Follow these precautions when visiting unfamiliar websites.

  • Never share OTPs, passwords or PINs.
  • Check the domain carefully for spelling changes.
  • Avoid downloading unknown files.
  • Do not make payments on suspicious websites.
  • HTTPS alone does not prove a website is legitimate.

Important Limitations

  • URL analysis does not guarantee that a website is safe or malicious.
  • A suspicious URL is assessed using available detection signals and rules.
  • Unless a live reputation service is integrated, external domain reputation is not independently verified.
  • ScamShield AI does not automatically open or interact with the submitted website.
  • Risk scores are automated assessments, not calibrated probabilities.
